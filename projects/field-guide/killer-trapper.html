The Trapper

Evan MacMillan

The Trapper is, you guessed it, a "Trap" killer. Use bear traps strategically placed to subdue your prey.

Power

Power: Bear Trap

  • You begin the trial with 2 Bear Traps equipped
  • 8 Disarmed Bear Traps spawn throughout the map
  • You can see the aura of all traps.

Interaction: Collecting Traps

  • Move placed traps by standing near them and pressing the interact button to retrieve them
  • By default, you can only carry 2 bear traps at a time

Interaction: Setting Traps

  • When carrying a Bear Trap, press and hold the power button to arm it
  • BUFF: Grants a 7.5% HASTE status for 5 seconds

Armed Traps (Survivor)

  • Stepping on a trap will immobilize the survivor
  • The survivor will become injured
  • Trapped survivors can attempt to free themselves, which can take a while
  • A trapped survivor can be grabbed by approaching them, then tapping your basic attack button
  • BONUS: If a survivor currently has endurance and is grabbed by a trap, make sure to GRAB them from the trap, and not just basic attack them. Grabbing will override endurance, but a basic attack will free them by only removing the endurance.

Armed Traps (Killer)

  • Stepping on a trap will temporarily stun you
  • NOTE: If carrying a survivor when you step in the trap, you will drop them.

Interaction: Disarming Traps

  • Survivors are able to disarm traps, but will have to perform an animation causing them to become stationary, and making them easier to attack
  • BONUS: If you catch a survivor off guard and they're trying to reset a trap, you can approach them and tap the basic attack button to attempt to grab them.

Interaction: Resetting Traps

  • Approach a disarmed trap, then press and hold the power button to reset it
  • Resetting a trap will re-arm it forcing survivors to disarm it again
